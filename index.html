Unfollow Queue for Instagram

Find out who you follow that doesn't follow you back, then unfollow them yourself in small batches, one tap at a time.

Your file is read inside this page and is not uploaded anywhere. The page remembers only which usernames you have clicked, in this browser.

How to get the export
  1. In Instagram, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions, then Export your information.
  2. Choose Create export, pick your account and select Export to device.
  3. Under Customize information, tick only Followers and following.
  4. Set the date range to All time and the format to JSON.
  5. Instagram emails you a link when the file is ready. This can take from a few minutes to a few hours.

Menu names change from time to time. The export is a snapshot, so request a new one whenever you want fresh numbers.

              Privacy policy

              Last updated 17 October 2026. This is a plain-language description of what this page does with your information.

              What you give the page

              You choose your Instagram data export (a ZIP, or the followers and following files from it). The page reads the file inside your own browser and compares the two lists there. The file and the usernames in it are never sent to the page's author or to any server run by the page.

              What the page stores

              The page saves the usernames you have clicked, chosen to keep or marked as not following or renamed, the time of each click, the profile counts you may type into "Check the numbers", and your settings (batch size, pause, order) in your browser's local storage on your own device. It does not store your lists, your export file or your Instagram login. There are no accounts, and the page does not set cookies or run analytics.

              You can delete the saved data at any time with "Reset progress" at the bottom of the queue, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

              Login details

              The page never asks for your Instagram username or password and never connects to your Instagram account. You do every unfollow yourself on Instagram.

              Other services involved

              To work, the page loads a ZIP-reading library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers receive your IP address and standard browser details when the page loads, as with any website, and handle them under their own policies. The company that hosts this page may also keep ordinary server logs.

              When you tap a username, Instagram opens in a new tab. Instagram's own terms and privacy policy apply there.

              Downloads, sharing and feedback

              The CSV files are created inside your browser from the lists the page already holds, and they are saved on your own device. They are not sent anywhere. The share buttons open WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook or X in a new tab, or your device's share menu, only when you click them, and those services handle your visit under their own policies. The feedback button opens a feedback form hosted by Zoho, which handles what you type there under its own policy. Nothing is sent unless you submit it. "Copy details for my message" copies counts and file names to your clipboard, never usernames, and only when you click it.

              Children

              The page is meant for people old enough to hold an Instagram account under Instagram's rules.
